# Birthday Template: config-driven HyperFrames composition

The first real template for the Video Presentation Generator. It renders a
complete birthday video (1920x1080, 30fps) from a single
`config.json`. No hand-editing HTML per person. The total length is
computed from the config: every card carries its own duration, defaulting
to 5s for the title, 5s per photo, 6s per collage scene, 6s per message
card, and 8s for the closing card, so it lands near a minute for typical
content and runs longer when more is switched on.

The composition canvas is natively 1920x1080: the whole design (layout,
typography, and GSAP motion offsets) is scaled 1.5x from the original 720p
design, so text and graphics render sharp at 1080p instead of being upscaled
by the encoder. Renders use the `delivery` quality preset.

## How it works

```
config.json  --(node generate.mjs)-->  index.html  --(hyperframes render)-->  renders/birthday-sample.mp4
```

- `config.json` is the only file you edit per video: celebrant name, the
  photo list with captions and per-photo settings, which photos go into
  the collage, the two greeting messages with signers, and the closing
  line and sign-off.
- `generate.mjs` reads the config, builds every scene, computes the timeline,
  and writes the final composition to `index.html`. It also writes
  `scenes.json` next to it: the total plus each scene's key, label, start,
  and duration, which the app's exact preview uses for its transport and
  filmstrip. The generated HTML carries a "Generated by generate.mjs"
  comment; edit the config or the generator, never the generated file.
- GSAP is loaded from a local copy at `assets/gsap.min.js`, so renders work
  offline with no CDN dependency.

## Fonts

Text comes in four slots, each with its own font pick in `config.json` under
`fonts`:

- `name`: the celebrant name on the title card (the split reveal chunks)
- `headings`: the title greeting line and eyebrow, the collage heading, and
  the closing line and eyebrow
- `captions`: photo captions and collage polaroid captions
- `messages`: message card text, the quote mark, and the signer lines

The closing sign-off keeps its original stack and is not part of the
slots.

Picks come from a curated pool defined in `generate.mjs`: `classic` (Georgia
serif), `clean` (Arial sans), `playfair` (Playfair Display), `fraunces`,
`dmserif` (DM Serif Display), `greatvibes` (Great Vibes), `caveat`, `bebas`
(Bebas Neue), `geist`, `jakarta` (Plus Jakarta Sans), `outfit`, and `dmsans`
(DM Sans). The defaults (`classic`, `classic`, `clean`, `classic`) reproduce
the template's original look. Unknown or missing keys fall back to the slot
defaults.

Picks beyond `classic` and `clean` load from Google Fonts: the generator
writes one stylesheet link covering exactly the selected families and
weights. That means **rendering with webfont picks needs an internet
connection**; without one, the text renders in the fallback stack named in
each pick (a similar serif, sans, or cursive). Script picks (`greatvibes`,
`caveat`) used for the name get an automatic size bump, since script faces
run small at the title size.

## Text effects

`config.textFx` can dress the text with a soft drop shadow, a warm gold
glow, and an outline, each with its own `on` flag and a 0 to 100 strength
(`width` for the outline). `scope` picks the slots: `display` covers the
name and headings slots, `all` adds captions and messages. The shadow and
glow merge into a single `text-shadow` per surface; the outline is drawn
with `-webkit-text-stroke`, off-white over the dark background presets
and deep ink over the paper cards and the light presets, with the name's
stroke clamped to 2px so script faces keep their counters open. All
effects default to off, which emits no extra CSS at all.

## Scene timing

The video is a title card, four middle blocks, and a closing card. The
middle blocks are the photo montage, the photo collage, the text collage,
and the message cards; they can play in any order (see Scene order
below). Every duration below is the default; each one can be changed in
the config (`titleDuration`, `photos[].duration`,
`collage.photo.duration`, `collage.text.duration`,
`messages[].duration`, `closingDuration`). Start times shift with the
order, with how much is switched on, and with the durations chosen. The
title and closing choreography stay anchored to the start of their card,
so extra seconds there are simply hold time.

| Scene | Duration | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Title card | 5s | "Happy Birthday," greeting words stagger up first, then the celebrant name arrives as a split reveal: the first chunk slides in from the left, the rest from the right, and they meet at center as one line. Gold rule grows once the name settles, background orbs drift |
| Photo montage | 5s per photo (35s with the sample 7) | One photo per beat, slow Ken Burns zoom with a brightness settle as each photo lands, soft vignette, caption bar slides up and the caption text follows. Between photos the picture fades through white (white ramps up over the last 0.35s and lifts over the first 0.35s of the next photo); the block edges keep a plain crossfade |
| Photo collage | 6s | Heading words stagger in, then the scene builds itself: 3 polaroid photos pop in one at a time about a second apart with a rotation settle, small gold hearts, stars, and dots pop in between them, the photo captions fade in late, then a gentle idle float. At other durations the whole build stretches or compresses to match |
| Text collage | 6s | Its own heading, then greeting cards: up to 4 paper cards build in one by one with a rotation settle, each showing a short greeting and who it is from, gold accents pop in between, then a gentle idle float. Timing scales with the duration like the photo collage |
| Message cards | 6s per card (12s with the sample 2) | Card rises with a slight tilt, quote mark pops in, then the message types on character by character (the typing window is the card duration minus 1.6s, so it always finishes before the card leaves), signer line slides in after the typing |
| Closing card | 8s | A scrapbook card lands at a slight tilt on the green background: warm paper with torn edges, two translucent tape strips settling onto its top corners. Closing line words stagger up on the paper, a deeper bronze rule grows, and the sign-off fades in last in italic serif like a handwritten note, held to the end |

With the sample config (default order, photo collage on, text collage
off) the timeline runs: title 0:00-0:05, montage 0:05-0:40, photo
collage 0:40-0:46, messages 0:46-0:58, closing 0:58-1:06, for a 66s
total. Turning the text collage on as well makes it 72s.

Sample content: a birthday video for Nur-Aina Ibrahim, with
messages from the YSE Family. Sample photos are seeded downloads from
picsum.photos (1280x720), stored in `assets/photos/photo-1.jpg` to
`photo-7.jpg`.

## Per-photo settings

Each entry in `photos` can carry its own display settings. All are
optional; the defaults reproduce the original montage exactly.

| Field | Default | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `duration` | 5 | 2-12 | Seconds this photo stays. The montage block is the sum of all photo durations, and each photo starts when the previous one ends |
| `zoom` | 115 | 100-150 | Ken Burns end scale in percent; the zoom starts at 105 and pushes in to this value |
| `focusX`, `focusY` | 50 | 0-100 | Position: which part of the photo stays in frame (see below) |
| `brightness` | 100 | 80-120 | Settled brightness in percent. The landing settle still plays, from 16 percent over this value down to it |
| `saturation` | 100 | 0-150 | Color saturation in percent; 0 is black and white |
| `frame` | `full` | see below | How the photo is presented |

Position picks which part of the photo stays in frame: Vertical at 100
keeps the BOTTOM of the photo visible (for a subject standing low in the
shot), Vertical at 0 keeps the top, and Horizontal works the same way
side to side. The same point is the zoom's center, so the Ken Burns push
moves toward the chosen area instead of drifting away from the subject.
It applies in every frame mode.

Frames:

- `full`: the original full-bleed photo with the caption bar at the
  bottom.
- `thin`: full-bleed plus a thin white border inset from the edges;
  caption bar as normal.
- `polaroid`: the photo becomes a large polaroid card centered on the
  scene background, tilted slightly (alternating direction per photo).
  The caption sits in the polaroid's bottom strip instead of the caption
  bar, and the Ken Burns zoom is clipped inside the photo area.
- `rounded`: the photo inset as a large rounded-corner card on the scene
  background with a soft shadow; the caption bar sits inside the card's
  bottom edge.
- `gold`: like `rounded`, but the card is a framed picture: a gold outer
  frame and an off-white mat around the photo, caption inside as with
  `rounded`.

Framed photos still fade through white between each other exactly like
full-bleed ones; the white layer covers the whole canvas.

## Paper styles

Message cards, text collage cards, and the closing card can each pick
a paper: `paper` on each `messages[]` entry, `paper` on each
`collage.text.cards[]` entry, and `closingPaper` at the top level.
Every paper stays light enough for the standard dark ink, except
`folder`, whose deeper manila tone switches the card's text to a dark
warm brown through the paper's own ink variables:

- `classic` (default): the original warm cream gradient with grain.
- `white`: clean near-white, minimal grain.
- `kraft`: warm tan kraft.
- `blush`: soft pink.
- `mint`: soft green.
- `sky`: soft blue.
- `crumpled`: warm white with crossing crease bands and a heavier
  grain, like a sheet smoothed back out.
- `folder`: manila folder tan with a raised tab along the top edge and
  a darker edge line; the ink turns deep brown (#3d2a10).
- `asymmetrical`: the classic warm paper under the closing card's torn,
  irregular outline; on message cards the tape strip tucks inside the
  safe area so the torn edge cannot cut it.
- `felt`: muted sage-grey-green with a dense, soft fibre noise; calm
  and matte.
- `specialty`: elegant ivory with a fine gold speckle and a thin double
  gold border inset, like formal stationery.

Unknown keys fall back to `classic`. A folder greeting card in the
text collage wears a smaller, scaled tab (a share of the card's width)
instead of the fixed tab the big cards use.

## Closing photo strips

The closing scene can also show small framed copies of the montage
photos in a strip above the scrap card, below it, or both.
`closingTopPhotos` and `closingBottomPhotos` are lists of photo indexes
(zero-based positions in the `photos` list); a photo may appear in both
strips. Each strip holds up to 5 photos: the prints pop in one by one
with gold accents between them (the top strip first, then the bottom
strip once the card has landed), and each print sways gently for the
rest of the scene. Out-of-range indexes are dropped and duplicates
collapse. Both lists empty (the default) leaves the closing scene
exactly as it was.

## Card effects

The title card, each message card, both collage scenes, and the closing
card can each carry an optional animated effect: a layer of small
animated assets over the scene backdrop, behind the card itself. Set
`titleEffect` at the top level, `effect` on each `messages[]` entry,
`effect` inside `collage.photo` and `collage.text`, and `closingEffect`
at the top level. Photo scenes have no effect. The choices:

- `none` (default everywhere): no effect layer.
- `confetti`: about 26 small pieces in gold, cream, sage, and blush fall
  the height of the frame with a sway and a spin.
- `sparkles`: about 14 gold four-point stars scattered over the frame,
  twinkling in place.
- `hearts`: about 12 hearts rise from the bottom edge to about 70% of
  the frame height, swaying and fading near the top.
- `petals`: about 14 soft petals in blush and cream drift down and
  sideways with a slow rotation.

Unknown keys fall back to `none`. Placement is pseudo-random but
deterministic (seeded from the card), so the same config always renders
the same layout. Every asset loops in whole cycles planned against its
scene's duration, so nothing animates past the end of its card.

## Scene order

`sceneOrder` in `config.json` lists the four middle block keys in play
order: `"photos"`, `"photoCollage"`, `"textCollage"`, `"messages"`. The
title card always plays first and the closing card always last. The
generator sanitizes the list: unknown keys are dropped, duplicates
collapse, and any block missing from the list is appended in the default
order (`photos`, `photoCollage`, `textCollage`, `messages`). A block
that is off or empty (no photos, no filled messages, a collage switched
off) contributes no scenes and no time.

## The two collage scenes

The photo collage and the text collage are separate scenes, and both can
appear in the same video:

- Photo collage (`collage.photo`): the 3 polaroids from
  `collage.photo.photoIndexes`, under `collage.photo.heading`. It is on
  unless `collage.photo.enabled` is set to `false`.
- Text collage (`collage.text`): greeting cards from
  `collage.text.cards`, up to 4 entries of `{ "text": "...", "from":
  "..." }`, each with an optional `paper` (see Paper styles), under
  its own heading `collage.text.heading`. Four cards
  land in a 2x2 grid, 3 cards sit in one row, and 2 in a single centered
  row, building in one by one. Card text uses the `messages` font slot
  and the signer line the `headings` slot. Cards with blank text are
  skipped. It is off unless `collage.text.enabled` is `true`.

The sample config carries 4 short greetings with the text collage
switched off, so setting `collage.text.enabled` to `true` is all it
takes to demo it.

## Backgrounds

The `background` key in `config.json` picks the backdrop for the title,
collage, message, and closing scenes. Eight presets ship in the
generator:

| Key | Type | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `deep-green` | Animated | The original: deep green with gold-green drifting orbs, cream words. |
| `emerald` | Animated | Richer green gradient, soft green orbs. |
| `midnight` | Animated | Deep navy to indigo, soft blue orbs. |
| `royal` | Animated | Deep purple gradient, violet orbs. |
| `sunset` | Animated | Plum dusk gradient, warm amber orbs. |
| `charcoal` | Color | Flat warm near-black, very faint static orbs. |
| `cream` | Color | Flat warm cream, words turn deep green, accents turn bronze. No orbs. |
| `sky` | Gradient | Pale blue gradient, words turn deep blue, subtle white orbs. |

Animated presets drift their orbs; the others hold still. Full-bleed
photo scenes never change; framed photos (polaroid, rounded, gold) sit
on the backdrop, and the paper cards (messages, text collage, closing)
keep their paper surface in every preset. An unknown
or missing key falls back to `deep-green`, so leaving the key out keeps
the original look.

## Music (optional, post-render step)

The render itself is silent. To add a background track, mux one onto the
finished MP4 with FFmpeg afterwards. This is the same step the app runs
automatically when a track is uploaded there.

```bash
ffmpeg -i renders/birthday-sample.mp4 -stream_loop -1 -i path/to/music.mp3 \
  -filter_complex "[1:a]volume=0.40,afade=t=in:st=0:d=1,afade=t=out:st=62.5:d=3.5[aout]" \
  -map 0:v -map "[aout]" -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k -t 66 \
  renders/birthday-with-music.mp4
```

How to read the parameters:

- `-stream_loop -1` loops the track if it is shorter than the video.
- `volume=0.40` is the track level, from 0 to 1 (0.40 means 40 percent).
- `afade=t=in:st=0:d=1` fades the music in over the first second.
- `afade=t=out:st=62.5:d=3.5` fades it out over the last 3.5 seconds. The
  start time is the video duration minus 3.5 (66 - 3.5 = 62.5 here);
  adjust it, and the final `-t 66`, if your video runs longer or shorter.
- The video stream is copied untouched (`-c:v copy`); only the audio is
  encoded, as AAC at 192k.

Only use music you have the right to use. The file
`assets/music-test.mp3` in this folder is a synthetic test tone track (a
generated sine chord pad) that exists only so this mux step can be
tested; it is not a bundled music library.

## Commands

Run from this folder. The `TMPDIR` and frames-cache settings are required on
this VM: `/tmp` has almost no free space and a default render aborts with
"Low disk space" (see `../SETUP.md`).

```bash
cd ~/workspace/video-presentation-generator/render-engine/birthday-template

# 1. Generate the composition from config.json
node generate.mjs

# 2. Validate (lint, runtime, layout, motion, contrast)
TMPDIR=$HOME/workspace/video-presentation-generator/render-engine/.tmp \
HYPERFRAMES_EXTRACT_CACHE_DIR=$HOME/workspace/video-presentation-generator/render-engine/.frames-cache \
../node_modules/.bin/hyperframes check

# 3. Render the MP4 (native 1920x1080, delivery quality preset)
TMPDIR=$HOME/workspace/video-presentation-generator/render-engine/.tmp \
HYPERFRAMES_EXTRACT_CACHE_DIR=$HOME/workspace/video-presentation-generator/render-engine/.frames-cache \
../node_modules/.bin/hyperframes render . -o renders/birthday-sample.mp4 --quality delivery --frames-cache-dir ../.frames-cache

# 4. Verify the output
ffprobe renders/birthday-sample.mp4
```

## Make a video for another person

1. Replace the files in `assets/photos/` with the new person's photos
   (1920x1080 or larger is ideal; smaller photos still work, the composition
   crops to fill and photos simply gain no extra detail).
2. Edit `config.json`:
   - `celebrantName`, `occasion`
   - `titleDate`: an optional date line printed under the title card's
     gold rule (free text, e.g. "October 22, 2026"); empty or missing
     means no date line
   - `titleDuration`, `closingDuration`: how long the title and closing
     cards stay (optional, defaults 5 and 8)
   - `photos`: one entry per montage photo, `file` path plus `caption`;
     any of the per-photo settings from the Per-photo settings section
     can be added per entry
   - `collage.photo.photoIndexes`: which photos (zero-based positions in
     the `photos` list) appear in the photo collage; exactly 3.
     `collage.photo.enabled` can be set to `false` to skip the scene,
     `collage.photo.heading` sets its heading, and
     `collage.photo.duration` its length (default 6)
   - `collage.text`: set `enabled` to `true` to add the text collage
     scene, with its own `heading`, up to 4 `{ text, from }` entries in
     `cards`, and a `duration` (default 6)
   - `sceneOrder`: the four middle block keys (`photos`,
     `photoCollage`, `textCollage`, `messages`) in the order they
     should play; the title and closing cards stay fixed at the ends
   - `messages`: entries of `text` plus `signedBy`, each with an
     optional `duration` (default 6) and `paper` (default `classic`)
   - `fonts`: one pick per text slot (`name`, `headings`, `captions`,
     `messages`) from the pool in the Fonts section above
   - `closingLine`, `closingSignOff`, `closingPaper`
   - `closingTopPhotos`, `closingBottomPhotos`: optional photo indexes
     for the small framed strips above and below the closing card (see
     Closing photo strips); up to 5 per strip, empty means no strip
3. Re-run `node generate.mjs`, then the render command above.
4. The new MP4 lands wherever you point `-o`; rename it per person, for
   example `renders/birthday-juan.mp4`.

Note: changing the number of photos or messages changes the total duration,
since photo and message durations add up (5s and 6s each by default, or
whatever their `duration` fields say). The generator prints the new total
when it runs.

## Known gaps (v1)

- **Music is a post-render step.** The composition itself stays silent;
  add a track with the FFmpeg mux in the Music section above (the app
  runs the same step automatically when a track is uploaded there).
- **Birthday template only.** Appreciation, Awards, and Retirement templates
  from the prototype are not built yet; they would follow this same
  config-to-composition pattern.
- Photos are cropped to fill their frame by the composition CSS. The
  per-photo Position fields (see Per-photo settings) choose which part
  of the photo the crop keeps, but there is no freehand crop or rotate.
- Sample photos are generic picsum images, not real celebrant photos.
