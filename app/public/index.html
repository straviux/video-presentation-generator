Birthday is ready to use. The other event types are on the way.

Title card

Everything for one card, gathered in one place. Pick a card in the filmstrip below the stage or in the Video summary, and its words, timing, paper, and effects all show here. Each card can also carry an optional animated effect: confetti, sparkles, hearts, or petals.

Use New card to add a photo, a message card, or a greeting card. The Move buttons change where the selected card plays; the title card always opens the video and the closing card always ends it.

Scene preset

A preset sets the order the scenes play in and which collages are on. It never changes your words, photos, papers, or timings. Save the arrangement you have now under a name to reuse it later. Shuffle rolls a new order, new card effects, or both; the title card always opens and the closing card always ends.

Fonts

Pick a different font for each kind of text in the video. Press Update preview above the stage to see your picks in the real design. The fonts load from Google Fonts, so the preview and the render need an internet connection; without one, each pick falls back to a similar system font.

Text effects

Optional shadow, glow, and outline for the text in the video. Display text covers the celebrant name and the headings; All text adds the photo captions and the message cards too. Each effect has its own switch and strength.

Music

Add one background music track to the finished video. It loops if it is shorter than the video, fades in at the start, and fades out over the last few seconds. Leave this empty and the video stays silent.

Only use music you have the right to use, for example your own recording or a track whose license covers videos like this one.

Background

The backdrop behind the title, collage, message, and closing scenes. Photos stay full screen in every backdrop, and the paper cards keep their paper.

Animated backdrops drift gently in the video. Framed photos (Polaroid, Rounded, Gold frame) sit on this backdrop; full-bleed photos fill the screen in every backdrop.

Projects

Save the whole editor, photos, music, and every setting included, under a name, then reopen it later exactly as you left it. Saving with the same name replaces the older copy. Projects are stored as files in the app's projects folder on this machine.
