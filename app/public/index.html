Photos

Add JPG, PNG, or WebP photos. Each photo gets 5 seconds in the montage. Use the arrows to change the order, and tick photos here or in the Collage tab for the photo collage.

Messages

Up to two message cards. Each one gets 6 seconds. Leave a message blank to skip it. Use the Scenes tab to choose where they play.

Collage

Two optional scenes, each 6 seconds. The photo collage shows 3 photos together as polaroids; the text collage shows greeting cards. Both can appear in the same video. Use the Scenes tab to choose where each one plays.

Scenes

The order the scenes play in the video. The title card always opens the video and the closing card always ends it. Use the arrows to move the middle scenes; a scene marked Off is skipped when the video is made.

Fonts

Pick a different font for each kind of text in the video. The stage preview follows your picks. The fonts load from Google Fonts, so the preview and the render need an internet connection; without one, each pick falls back to a similar system font.

Music

Add one background music track to the finished video. It loops if it is shorter than the video, fades in at the start, and fades out over the last few seconds. Leave this empty and the video stays silent.

Only use music you have the right to use, for example your own recording or a track whose license covers videos like this one.
