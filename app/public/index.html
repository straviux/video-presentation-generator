Photos

Add JPG, PNG, or WebP photos. Every photo has its own controls under it: how long it stays, its frame, and its Position. Position picks which part of the photo stays in frame, so a subject low in the shot is not cropped out. Use the arrows to change the order, and tick photos here or in the Collage tab for the photo collage.

Messages

Add up to six message cards. Set how long each card stays, which paper it is written on, and an optional animated effect. Leave a message blank to skip it. The buttons under a card move it, copy it, or remove it. Use the Scenes tab to choose where the messages play.

Scenes

The order the scenes play in the video. The title card always opens the video and the closing card always ends it. Use the arrows to move the middle scenes; a scene marked Off is skipped when the video is made.

Fonts

Pick a different font for each kind of text in the video. Press Update preview above the stage to see your picks in the real design. The fonts load from Google Fonts, so the preview and the render need an internet connection; without one, each pick falls back to a similar system font.

Text effects

Optional shadow, glow, and outline for the text in the video. Display text covers the celebrant name and the headings; All text adds the photo captions and the message cards too. Each effect has its own switch and strength.

Music

Add one background music track to the finished video. It loops if it is shorter than the video, fades in at the start, and fades out over the last few seconds. Leave this empty and the video stays silent.

Only use music you have the right to use, for example your own recording or a track whose license covers videos like this one.

Background

The backdrop behind the title, collage, message, and closing scenes. Photos stay full screen in every backdrop, and the paper cards keep their paper.
